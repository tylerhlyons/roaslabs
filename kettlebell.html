Pick a group

Choose 1–2 muscle groups per session. Don't try to hit all six at once — you'll just burn out.

02

Run 3–4 moves

Pick 3–4 exercises from the group, run them in order, and rest as listed between sets.

03

Progress weekly

Week 1: the listed reps. Week 2: add a rep per set. Week 3: add weight or another set.
