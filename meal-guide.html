How to use it

Each meal lands around 50–60g of protein and ~370–640 calories. Don't overthink it — get your protein in, build your day from these, and repeat what works.

01

Hit protein first

Protein is the muscle-saver and the appetite-killer. Lock it in at every meal and the rest gets easy.

02

Swap to taste

Beef, turkey, chicken and steak are interchangeable. Use what's at the station and what you actually like.

03

Build your day

Stack 3–4 of these to hit your daily target. Bigger guy? Add a scoop of rice or potatoes for more fuel.

04

Prep for the shift

Most of these batch and reheat. Cook once, eat through the 24, and skip the drive-thru at 2am.
