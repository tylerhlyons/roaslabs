The free routine

10 minutes. 9 moves.
Do it post-shift or before bed.

No equipment. Move slow, breathe, and don't force anything. This is the exact sequence — run it top to bottom.

  1. 01

    Cat–Cow

    SPINE · 10 SLOW REPS

    On hands and knees. Arch and round the spine slowly with your breath. Wakes up a stiff back before anything else.

  2. 02

    World's Greatest Stretch

    HIPS + T-SPINE · 5 / SIDE

    Deep lunge, drop the elbow inside the front foot, then rotate and reach to the ceiling. The best single move for a beat-up body.

  3. 03

    90/90 Hip Switch

    HIPS · 8 / SIDE

    Sit with both knees bent at 90°, rotate side to side under control. Unlocks the hips that sitting in the rig locks down.

  4. 04

    Couch Stretch

    HIP FLEXORS + QUADS · 60s / SIDE

    Back knee down, shin up a wall or couch. Squeeze the glute and stand tall. This is where most back pain actually comes from.

  5. 05

    Thread the Needle

    UPPER BACK · 8 / SIDE

    From hands and knees, reach one arm under and through, then open up to the ceiling. Frees the shoulders and mid-back.

  6. 06

    Door-Frame Chest Stretch

    CHEST + SHOULDERS · 30s / SIDE

    Forearm on the door frame, step through gently. Undoes the hunched-forward posture from driving and desk time.

  7. 07

    Standing Forward Fold

    HAMSTRINGS + LOW BACK · 45s

    Soft knees, hinge at the hips and hang heavy. Let the head and arms go loose. Decompresses the whole back line.

  8. 08

    Glute Bridge

    GLUTES · 12 REPS

    On your back, drive through the heels and squeeze at the top. Wakes up the muscles that should be protecting your spine.

  9. 09

    Chin Tucks + Neck Release

    NECK · 10 REPS

    Gently draw the chin straight back, then slow tilts ear-to-shoulder. Kills the tension headaches and stiff neck.

How to actually use it

  • ✓ Run it once a day — post-shift or right before bed works best.
  • ✓ Slow and controlled beats deep and forced. Never stretch into sharp pain.
  • ✓ Consistency > intensity. 10 minutes daily for 2 weeks and you'll feel the difference.
  • ✓ Hurting more than it should? That's a signal — not something to push through.
